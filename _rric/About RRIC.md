<p>Robotics Research and Innovation Centre: Pioneering Robotics Innovation
Since April 2020</p>
<p>Established in April 2020 to spearhead state-of-the-art applied research
in robotics technology.</p>
<p>Brings together NP’s multidisciplinary expertise in robotics research
to co-create robotics solutions with businesses and public organisations</p>
<p>Underscores NP’s commitment to nurture a strong pipeline of robotics talent,
and ensure that staff stay abreast of industry needs</p>
<p>Supports the national innovation agenda in Singapore’s journey towards
being a Smart Nation.</p>
