<p>Supported by Enterprise Singapore, the Advanced Manufacturing Centre of
Innovation (AMCOI) advances manufacturing innovation by harnessing the
expertise of Ngee Ann Polytechnic, Singapore Polytechnic, Nanyang Polytechnic,
Temasek Polytechnic, and Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology
(SIMTech).&nbsp;</p>
